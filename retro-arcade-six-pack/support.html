Retro Arcade Six Pack Support

Help for Brick Breaker, Snake Circuit, Neon Paddle, Target Rush, Memory Grid and Sky Hopper.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email magnetic.axis@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version when reporting a technical problem.

Common questions

  • Change difficulty: open a game and choose Easy, Medium or Hard before starting.
  • Mute music: tap the speaker button in a game.
  • Wind-Down Mode: tap the timer button to gradually slow and dim a session before it finishes.
  • Remove ads: open Settings and choose Remove Ads. This is a one-time purchase.
  • Restore a purchase: open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple ID used for the original purchase.
