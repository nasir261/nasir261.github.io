Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy applies to Retro Arcade Six Pack. The app is free to download and is supported by advertising, with an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements.

Advertising services

We use Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, these services may collect:

  • Coarse location estimated from an IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as advertisements viewed
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches, taps, video views or ad interactions
  • Crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data

This information may be used for third-party advertising, analytics and app functionality. Some information may be linked to a device or used for tracking as described by Google's SDK privacy manifest.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. Advertising Privacy Choices are available in Settings when required. Removing ads prevents future advertisements from being requested after the purchase is recognised.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, star ratings, difficulty choices, tutorial state and audio settings are stored locally. The app does not require a name, email address, account, profile or login.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card or bank-account details. Purchase entitlement information is used only to unlock or restore the ad-free experience. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-party policies and retention

Google controls retention and processing for information collected by its advertising services. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, advertising services or legal requirements change. Questions can be sent to magnetic.axis@gmail.com.
