Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. This policy describes the LarkNova app and its support website.

This policy describes the mobile Solitaire:LarkNova game published by Muhammad Ali. Contact magnetic.axis@gmail.com about privacy.

Your game stays on your device

The game saves your cards, moves, preferences, statistics, achievements and age declaration locally on your device. We do not operate a game account system or cloud leaderboard. This information is not sent to a game server. Device backup behaviour may depend on your operating system. Removing the app or clearing its storage removes the local game data; an operating-system backup may retain a separate copy.

Advertisements

The native apps use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process IP addresses, approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. Data is used to provide and measure advertisements, operate the services, and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads, which still involve data processing.

Where applicable, Google's consent message lets you make advertising choices. Open Settings → Privacy → Advertising choices to revisit available choices. Ads are requested only when the consent SDK permits them. The game remains playable if advertisements are unavailable.

Google's handling, retention and international processing of data are described in its Privacy Policy and information about partner apps and websites. Changing an advertising choice does not automatically delete information previously processed by Google. Device advertising controls may also be available in system settings.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond and investigate the problem. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Website hosting

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under its privacy statement. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to these pages.

Retention and international processing

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system backups. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Google and GitHub manage their own service retention and may process information outside your country under the safeguards described in their policies.

Your choices and requests

You can change in-game settings, stop playing, clear local storage or uninstall the app. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, withdraw consent or object to certain processing. Contact the address above; requests involving Google's services may also need to be directed to Google. You may complain to your relevant data-protection authority.

Audience and updates

Solitaire:LarkNova is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. A local age declaration is requested before gameplay and advertising-consent requests. If a user declares they are under 18, access is blocked. We do not collect a date of birth or identity document; this is a declaration, not verified proof of age. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Material changes to the released policy will be reflected here with an updated date.
