A little help with your game

Solitaire:LarkNova is in development for adults aged 18+. Store downloads will be linked here after release.

Contact support

Email nasiralimd@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version and a short description of the problem. A screenshot can help; please remove personal information first.

How do I move cards?

Tap a card or sequence, then its destination, or drag it. Build tableau columns in descending order with alternating colours. Build foundations from Ace to King in the same suit. Only a King can start an empty column.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Core gameplay works offline. Ads and advertising privacy messages require an internet connection. Your game stays available when an ad cannot load.

How do I change draw mode or scoring?

Open Settings to choose one-card or three-card draws, then start a new deal. The game uses standard Klondike scoring only. It has no simulated gambling, wagers, stakes, cash value, prizes or payouts.

Are all games winnable?

The labelled verified deals have saved solutions. Random shuffles are not guaranteed to be solvable. Daily deals rotate through a library of 32 verified deals. Guided replay is practice and does not award wins or XP.

Where is my progress?

Progress is stored on the same device. There is no account or cross-device synchronisation. Clearing app data may permanently remove it.

How do I change advertising choices?

Open Settings → Privacy → Advertising choices. The options Google provides depend on your region and current consent status.

Can I remove ads?

This version is free with banner advertisements. There is no purchase or subscription to remove them.
