Princess Orbit privacy policy

Updated 23 October 2026. Developer: Muhammad Ali. Contact: magnetic.axis@gmail.com.

Game records

No game account is needed. Best scores, sound and haptic preferences and ad-frequency counters are stored locally. You can reset scores in Settings. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to Apple device backup settings. The developer does not receive these game records.

Advertising

The free game uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent-management service for menu banners and occasional ads at mission breaks. Google and its advertising partners may process IP-derived general location, device identifiers, ad interactions, crash information, diagnostic and performance data to deliver, measure, secure and improve advertising. Non-personalised advertising can still process data.

Where required, privacy choices are requested before ads. An advertising privacy-options control appears in the app’s privacy screen and Settings when required. The app requests Apple’s tracking permission before starting advertising when it has not been decided. You can decline and keep playing. Permission may also be changed in Apple Settings. Ads are restricted to Google’s general content rating; the game is intended for a general audience and is not enrolled in Apple’s Kids category.

Optional Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is a single, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple processes payment. The app reads Apple-verified purchase information to provide and restore ad removal. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Use Restore Purchases in the shop with the Apple Account used to buy it. Removing ads stops new ad requests, but does not delete information previously processed by providers. A refund or revocation may remove the entitlement.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include. We use it to investigate and answer your request, and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, banking information or sensitive personal information.

Your choices, retention and rights

Local records remain until cleared or the app is removed; backups are controlled through your device and Apple settings. Provider retention differs by data and purpose: see Google retention information, Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent, subject to applicable exceptions. Contact magnetic.axis@gmail.com about information held by the developer. Provider policies explain requests about their information. UK users can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Updates

We will update this policy if the app’s practices or providers change.
