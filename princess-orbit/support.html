How do I rescue?

Every mode begins with its own guide. Drag diamond relay A or B vertically, or use its height slider. Higher percentages raise the buoy. The full amber cable shows your route; a pink crossing means it touches a moon.

In Tether Charts, Drift Watch and Relay Duel, send the spool while Mira raises her hand. Once she boards, tap Engage winch near 35% tension and Release near 65%. These are toggle buttons, not press-and-hold controls. Keep the gauge between 22% and 70%; tension above 90% for too long snaps the cable. HOME 100% completes the rescue and awards points. Mira stays safely at her station after a failed attempt.

Which mode should I choose?

  • Tether Charts: six puzzles, three spools and a manual winch.
  • Quiet Orbit: three easy, preplotted routes, unlimited retries, no signal wait and an automatic winch. Start here to learn.
  • Drift Watch: 90 seconds to complete as many rescues as possible across three repeating storm routes with moving moons and dock.
  • Relay Duel: two players pass the device. Each gets one 45-second attempt on each of three matching charts; highest total wins. Pass the phone at the handover card.

Pause stops motion and all timers. Handover and starting guides also stop timers. Quiet Orbit manages tension automatically; other modes need your winch taps.

How do I remove ads?

Open Remove Ads from the mission menu or Settings. This is a one-time purchase with a UK target price of £1.99, with the actual local price displayed by the App Store. No subscription. All four game modes are free to play.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought Remove Ads. If the store is unavailable, keep playing and retry later. Apple handles charges and refunds; the developer cannot view your payment-card details.

Ad and privacy choices

Ads appear on the menu or at occasional mission breaks. They do not interrupt a capsule journey. Required regional privacy options are available in Settings and the privacy screen. You can decline Apple’s tracking permission and continue playing. No ad needs to load for gameplay to work offline.

Sound, pauses and saved scores

Help messages are text-only. Settings controls optional effects/haptics and resets best scores. Menus and leaving the app pause a mission; tap Resume afterwards. Scores and chart seals are local to the device. Build6 starts a fresh record table for the changed rules; previous build5 and arcade records remain stored separately.
