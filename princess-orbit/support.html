Princess Orbit support

For help, email magnetic.axis@gmail.com. Include your app version, device/iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I rescue?

Choose a mode, then wait for her hand. Pull back from Earth’s rocket to set angle and power, and release during the wave. The sliders and launch button offer an alternative. Earth’s gravity bends the flight; anticipate the moving centre portal. Use the faded miss trail to improve. A valid launch remains valid after the hand lowers.

How do I remove ads?

Open Remove Ads from the mission menu or Settings. This is a one-time purchase with a UK target price of £1.99, with the actual local price displayed by the App Store. No subscription. All four game modes are free to play.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought Remove Ads. If the store is unavailable, keep playing and retry later. Apple handles charges and refunds; the developer cannot view your payment-card details.

Ad and privacy choices

Ads appear on the menu or at occasional mission breaks. They do not interrupt a rocket flight. Required regional privacy options are available in Settings and the privacy screen. You can decline Apple’s tracking permission and continue playing. No ad needs to load for gameplay to work offline.

Sound, pauses and saved scores

Help messages are text-only. Settings controls optional effects/haptics and resets best scores. Menus and leaving the app pause a mission; tap Resume afterwards. Scores are local to the device.
