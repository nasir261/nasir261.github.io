Beacon’s Ascent Support

Beacon’s Ascent is a calm, offline lighthouse adventure for iPhone and iPad.

Getting started

Choose a lighthouse keeper, begin the journey and step onto the dock. Follow the current task and harbour checklist. Move, jump and interact to collect supplies, repair the route and climb the lighthouse.

Comfort and progress

The game has no lives, countdown or game-over screen. Friendly checkpoints return the keeper safely after a missed jump. Reduce Motion and Larger Text are available on the keeper-selection screen.

Help

If the game does not respond, close and reopen it. For further help, email magnetic.axis@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description of the issue.
